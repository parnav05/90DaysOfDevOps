## My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud

DevOps is a culture and engineering practice that bridges the gap between development and operations by enabling **automation, collaboration, monitoring, and continuous delivery**. The goal is to release software **faster, reliably, and repeatedly**.

Cloud engineering provides the **scalable infrastructure** (compute, storage, networking) on which DevOps practices run efficiently. Cloud + DevOps together allow teams to build, deploy, and manage applications without manual intervention.
